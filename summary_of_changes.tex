\begin{center}
{\LARGE\bfseries Summary of Changes}\par
\vspace{0.5em}
{\large TIFO: Time-Invariant Frequency Operator for Stationarity-Aware Representation Learning in Time Series}
\end{center}

We thank the reviewers and program committee for their constructive feedback.
The revision prioritizes reproducible evidence, fair baseline comparisons, and
claims that match the observed results. The completed changes are:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=0.65em]
  \item \textbf{Reproducible matched evaluation.}
  We rebuilt the experiment path around one historical TIFO implementation and
  a unified Ori--TIFO switch. Every promoted run now records its exact command,
  seed, dataset hash, code state, log, and final metrics. Results are reported
  over seeds 2021--2023 and include the original backbone as a matched control.

  \item \textbf{Replaced unsupported headline results and validated reconstruction.}
  The previous large-gain table could not be traced to complete multi-seed
  artifacts. We replaced it with mechanically generated evidence in
  Table~\ref{table:verified_main_results}: PatchTST over seven datasets and four
  horizons, and iTransformer over seven datasets at $H=96$. The revision reports
  both improvements and negative cases, including the Traffic limitation. A
  validation-only gate also tested a Hermitian rFFT/iRFFT implementation; the
  selected ETTh1 and ETTm2 settings were frozen before three-seed evaluation.

  \item \textbf{Added recent baselines.}
  Table~\ref{table:recent_baselines} adds ACN (ICML 2025) and WDAN (2025),
  two recent model-agnostic normalization plug-ins, across all seven datasets
  under the same iTransformer, $H=96$, three-seed protocol. Their official
  dataset-level settings are retained; missing Traffic settings are selected
  on validation data only. Because the official repositories use different
  training engines, we reran a bare iTransformer inside each engine. In these
  paired controls, TIFO and ACN improve 6/7 datasets, while TIFO has the
  smallest worst-case degradation (1.02\%) and effect variance.

  \item \textbf{Calibrated empirical claims.}
  We removed the unverified 33.3\%/55.3\% gains, the universal-improvement
  statement, and the unsupported top-result count from the abstract,
  contributions, results discussion, and conclusion. The new wording states
  the verified gains, mixed outcomes, and method boundary explicitly.

  \item \textbf{Separated verified and pending evidence.}
  Historical tables whose configurations or artifacts are incomplete are no
  longer compiled into the revised manuscript. Only mechanically aggregated,
  traceable values are eligible for the final paper tables.

  \item \textbf{Added mechanism and controlled-shift diagnostics.}
  Across frozen ETTh1 and ETTm2 checkpoints, learned spectral gains are
  positively associated with the precomputed stationarity scores. We report
  this as empirical alignment rather than a monotonic guarantee. A separate
  three-seed stress test coherently amplifies high-frequency input/future
  components. TIFO's relative effect improves monotonically with shift strength
  on both ETTh1 and Traffic, although Traffic remains an absolute negative case.
\end{enumerate}
