# KDD 2027 Cycle 1 rules for this paper

Official sources:

- Research Track CFP: <https://kdd2027.kdd.org/research-track-call-for-papers/>
- Author FAQ: <https://kdd2027.kdd.org/frequently-asked-questions/>
- Submission venue: <https://openreview.net/group?id=KDD.org%2F2027%2FResearch_Track_Cycle_1>

Recheck these pages before final submission because venue rules can change.

## Deadlines

- Abstract: 19 July 2026, end of day Anywhere on Earth.
- Full paper: 26 July 2026, end of day Anywhere on Earth.
- Rebuttal: 29 September–13 October 2026.
- Notification: 14 November 2026.

Deadlines are strict. Do not use a placeholder abstract or make a large title/abstract change after the abstract deadline.

## Resubmission eligibility and form

This paper is eligible because it received **Resubmit** in KDD 2026 Cycle 2.

- Set `Resubmission Flag` to `Yes`.
- Enter the previous forum URL: `https://openreview.net/forum?id=2b776YkXBX`.
- Include a one-page Summary of Changes as the first PDF page.
- Do not exceed one summary page.
- Previous submission and reviews are visible to the KDD reviewers/AC/SAC through the linked forum.
- Do not append the exported review PDF.
- Do not color revised text or add a change list elsewhere in the paper.

## Manuscript

- English, ACM double-column format.
- Recommended class: `\documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}`.
- One PDF.
- Eight self-contained content pages, followed by references and an optional unlimited appendix.
- Double-blind: remove names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and identifying language; refer to prior work in the third person.
- Preprints are allowed; the anonymized manuscript need not cite the authors’ own preprint.
- An anonymized code repository may be cited. Rebuttal/discussion cannot be used to add links after the deadline.

## Authors and OpenReview

- Finalize the full author list and order by the abstract deadline.
- Each author needs a complete OpenReview profile: current and past affiliations for at least five years, homepage, DBLP when applicable, ORCID, advisors, and recent publications; add other relations where applicable.
- Incomplete profiles are grounds for desk rejection.
- Maximum seven Research Track submissions per author per cycle.
- Nominate at least one qualified author-reviewer; if none meets the stated threshold, nominate the best-qualified author.

## Originality and conduct

- Do not place the same or substantially similar manuscript under concurrent review at another archival peer-reviewed venue.
- Submit to one KDD track only; there are no track transfers in the cycle.
- Declare conflicts accurately in OpenReview.
- Treat artifact pledges as commitments; acceptance may be retracted if a pledge is later broken.
